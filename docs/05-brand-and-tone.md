# Brand and tone

- Look: professional, calm, maritime. Deep navy and a warm amber accent, lots of white space, large photos of shipyards and vessels in Sibu and the Rejang river. Not flashy.
- Suggested colours: navy `#1F4E79`, blue `#5B8DB8`, pale blue `#DCE6F1`, amber `#B45309`, ink `#1F2937` `[TBC; owner may have a logo and palette]`
- Type: clean sans-serif, large body text (this audience reads on phones and tablets). 
- Tone: plain, confident, factual. Short sentences. No hype, no buzzwords, no "world-class".
- Language: English first. Keep company and event names exactly as in `docs/01-event-facts.md`.
- Always show the relationship: "Road to Maritime Expo Asia 2027" on the March page; "Hosted by SAMIN, co-organised by MarineX International".
- Logos: SAMIN and MarineX logos go in `assets/` `[TBC; get written permission to use the SAMIN logo]`.
- Images: use only photos we own or have permission for. Mark placeholders clearly.
